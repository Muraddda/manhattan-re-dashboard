# Style reference
Source: https://dribbble.com/shots/27409850-Logistics-Dashboard-UI-UX-Supply-Chain-Platform ("Logixor" by Shahed Mahmud).
Files: shot-1.jpg (monitor mockup), shot-2.png (framed UI), shot-3.png (full UI).

- Palette: near-white / pale blue-gray (#eef2f8) base; royal blue primary (#2f6bff); orange secondary (#ff8a3d); green (up/good), red (down/alert), purple accents; navy-black text, cool gray secondary text.
- Background / hero: full-bleed isometric 3D scene (warehouses, trucks) rendered almost monochrome white/pale blue with blue accents, soft lavender-blue haze fading toward edges, so cards float on top of it.
- Panels: floating white cards, ~20px radius, thin white border, very soft blue-gray shadow; inner rows are white tiles with 1px light borders and ~14px radius.
- Top bar: logo left; segmented pill nav with the active item a filled royal-blue pill; round white icon buttons right (search, bell with red count badge, chat, theme) + avatar.
- Cards: "Command Overview" KPI list with pastel-tinted square icon tiles, value right-aligned, small colored deltas (↑ green / ↓ red); "System Alerts" list with colored icons + timestamps and a 3-column mini-KPI strip; bottom row of four equal cards.
- Charts: thin line chart (blue + orange, white-filled dot markers, light gridlines, floating tooltip card); vertical bar chart with blue gradient bars and the highlight bar orange-hatched; thin progress bars.
- Typography: clean geometric sans (Inter/SF-like), medium weights, ~15px card titles, 11px gray labels, large semibold numbers.
- Spacing: generous 16px gutters, airy padding, ample whitespace; accents used sparingly (orange progress callout floating in the scene).
